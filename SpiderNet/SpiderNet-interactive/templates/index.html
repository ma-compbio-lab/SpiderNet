{# -- Long-form intro ------------------------------------------------- #}

Each meta-interaction represents a latent, coordinated communication module defined over ordered sender–receiver cell pairs, linking sender regulators, ligand–receptor bridges, and receiver targets. By jointly modeling co-expression and gene expression across ordered pairs, SpiderNet anchors latent communication modules to both intercellular signaling and intracellular transcriptional programs.

The result is a compact, reusable, biologically interpretable basis for tissue communication — supporting cross-context comparison, in silico perturbation, and detection of multi-hop cascades across cell chains.

Validated across simulations and diverse spatial transcriptomics datasets — aging brain, cancer, and perturbational settings — SpiderNet provides a general framework for comparing, perturbing, and tracing multicellular regulatory architecture in situ.
