SpiderNet: Interpretable modeling of intercellular meta-interactions

SpiderNet is an interpretable framework that learns cell-cell meta-interaction representations from spatial transcriptomics data as directional modules of multicellular regulation. Each meta-interaction represents a latent, coordinated communication module defined over ordered sender-receiver cell pairs, linking sender regulators, ligand-receptor bridges, and receiver targets.

By jointly modeling ligand-receptor co-expression and gene expression across ordered cell pairs, SpiderNet anchors latent communication modules to both intercellular signaling and intracellular transcriptional programs. This yields a compact, reusable, and biologically interpretable basis of tissue communication, enabling cross-context communication program comparison, in silico perturbational interrogation of neighboring-cell effects, and detection of multi-hop communication cascades across cell chains.

Validated across simulations and diverse real spatial transcriptomics datasets spanning aging brain, cancer, and perturbational settings, SpiderNet provides a general framework for comparing, perturbing, and tracing multicellular regulatory architecture in situ across tissue contexts.

Analysis Workflows

Basic Analysis

Explore fundamental patterns in meta-interactions:

  • MI Correlation: Relationships among inferred meta-interactions
  • LR Loading: Ligand-receptor pathway enrichment for each MI
  • Cell-Type Pairs: MI enrichment across sender-receiver pairs
  • MI Patterns: Overall MI intensity and distribution
Subtype Analysis

Discover cell subtypes guided by meta-interactions:

  • Cell Subtypes: MI-guided malignant cell subtype identification
  • Neighborhood: Analysis of neighboring cell type proportions
  • Functional States: Association with biological processes (angiogenesis, hypoxia, etc.)
  • CAF Markers: Cancer-associated fibroblast program analysis
  • Clinical Data: Correlation with stage, site, and outcomes
MI Cascade Analysis

Detect higher-order multi-hop cell communication:

  • MI Colocalization: Identify significant upstream-downstream MI pairs
  • Cell-Type Triplets: Characterize cell1 → cell2 → cell3 cascades
  • Spatial Visualization: In-situ MI cascade patterns in tissue
  • LR Pathways: Ligand-receptor activity in cascades
  • Gene Programs: Downstream transcriptional responses and GO enrichment

Getting Started

Running SpiderNet

Run SpiderNet on your spatial transcriptomics data to generate analysis results: 

cd SpiderNet
conda activate spidernet
python run_spidernet.py \
  --data your_dataset.h5ad \
  --output Results/
