Main Publication

SpiderNet: Interpretable modeling of intercellular meta-interactions

A framework for learning cell-cell meta-interaction representations from spatial transcriptomics data as directional modules of multicellular regulation. Each meta-interaction represents a latent, coordinated communication module defined over ordered sender-receiver cell pairs, linking sender regulators, ligand-receptor bridges, and receiver targets.

Authors:
Junjie Tang, Shaoheng Liang, Shahul Alam, Wenduo Cheng, Jian Ma

Status:
